These aren't corporate problems. They're church problems — with higher emotional stakes, fewer HR guardrails, and a mission too important to let slide.

The one place that should be the healthiest, most aligned team in the room — the church staff — is often running on low trust, unclear roles, and unspoken tension. And the damage is slow, quiet, and cumulative.

01
🧭
Symptom 01

Vision Drift

What it looks like

Departments pulling in different directions. New staff never truly absorbed the culture. Language around "why we exist" has gone stale — and nobody's noticed.

The risk: Without a shared north star, energy fragments and your church's distinctiveness fades.

02
⛪
Symptom 02

Ministry Silos

What it looks like

Turf wars over budget, volunteers, and calendar. "My ministry, my rooms." Worship vs. children's vs. youth — competing islands instead of one body.

The risk: Outward impact stalls when the parts of the body stop communicating.

03
🤝
Symptom 03

The Trust Deficit

What it looks like

Passive agreement in meetings. Real concerns shared in the hallway. Gossip dressed as prayer requests. A growing sense of church "politics."

The risk: You can't build a church on a team performing unity while living in suspicion.

04
📋
Symptom 04

Leadership Without Clarity

What it looks like

Role confusion, accountability gaps, senior leaders as bottlenecks. Staff unsure who to go to — decisions feel slow, arbitrary, or political.

The risk: When no one knows who owns what, God-given opportunities are squandered.

05
💚
Symptom 05

Staff Health in Decline

What it looks like

Your best people are quietly leaving. Burnout masked as busyness. A growing gap between what staff say publicly and what they feel privately.

The risk: You can't lead a healthy congregation from an unhealthy staff room.
